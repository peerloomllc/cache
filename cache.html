About Cache
Design Philosophy
Cache is a single HTML file with zero dependencies. No build system, no frameworks, no servers, no accounts, no tracking. Open it in any browser, on any OS.

Your financial data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Back up by exporting JSON; migrate by copying the HTML file and your export.

Simple. Private. Durable.

    Balance Bookmarklet
    Update account balances without manually typing — log into your bank, click the bookmarklet, and Cache captures the balance automatically.
    Step 1 — Install once
    Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
    🔖 Cache Balance
    Works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Arc, and Brave.
    Step 2 — First-time setup per account
    First, expand an account card and add your institution's Login URL (e.g. https://chase.com/login) in the Login URL field.

    Then click ↻ next to the URL. Cache will open that page in a new tab.

    Log in, navigate to the page showing your balance, then click Cache Balance in your bookmarks bar.

    A green outline will appear as you hover — click on your account balance. Cache remembers that element for next time.
    Step 3 — Ongoing updates
    Log into your bank → click Cache Balance in your bookmarks bar. The balance syncs instantly — no clicking required after first-time setup.
    Re-pick an element
    If a bank redesigns their site and the selector breaks, delete the saved selector from your bank's browser storage (DevTools → Application → Local Storage → cache-balance-sel) and run the bookmarklet again to re-pick.
